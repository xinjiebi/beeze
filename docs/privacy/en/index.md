# Beeze Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 24, 2026**<br>
**Effective date: November 24, 2026**

Welcome to Beeze. Beeze is a local music player provided by **XJBI** (“we,” “us,” or “our”).

We value your privacy. This policy explains how Beeze processes your local music and related data, which system permissions it requests, and how advertising services may process data.

**Beeze does not require an account or provide cloud storage, and does not upload your songs, lyrics, artwork, listening history, liked songs, or custom playlists to the developer. Beeze includes Google AdMob advertising and related consent services. Google and its partners may process data as described in Section 3.**

## 1. Information We Process

To provide local music playback and management features, Beeze processes the following information locally on your device:

1. **Local audio files and metadata**
   - This includes audio files, song titles, artists, albums, artwork, duration, and related information.
   - It is used to scan your music library, display songs, search, sort, and play music.

2. **Local lyrics files**
   - With your authorization, Beeze reads `.lrc` lyrics files located in the same directories as your songs.
   - These files are used to display static and synchronized lyrics.

3. **Data created within the app**
   - This includes recently played records, liked songs, custom playlists, the playback queue, custom song display names, scan exclusion records, and related settings.
   - It is used to provide recently played, favorites, playlist management, display-name customization, and song-filtering features.

The developer does not remotely collect or obtain this local music and personal content, and Beeze does not proactively provide it to advertising partners for matching with your music.

## 2. Data Storage and Network Services

- Songs, lyrics, artwork, listening history, liked songs, custom playlists, and scan exclusion records are stored only on your device.
- Beeze requests Android’s INTERNET permission to load this privacy policy, request and display ads, and provide advertising consent features.
- Beeze does not use this permission to upload your songs, lyrics, artwork, listening history, playlists, or other local music content to the developer.
- When the privacy policy is loaded, GitHub Pages receives standard network request information, such as your IP address, as necessary to deliver the page. GitHub processes this information under its own privacy policy.
- Android cloud backup of Beeze’s app data is disabled.
- Your original audio and lyrics files remain in their original locations and will not be deleted when you uninstall Beeze.

## 3. Advertising Services and Privacy Consent

Beeze uses the **Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob)** to provide banner, native, and app-open ads, and the **Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)** to obtain and manage advertising privacy consent where legally required.

Google and its advertising partners may process the following information in connection with advertising and consent services:

- IP address, device model, operating system, language, and general device information;
- Android advertising ID or other device identifiers, depending on system settings and availability;
- Ad requests, impressions, clicks, and other advertising interactions;
- Approximate location, such as location inferred from an IP address;
- Information related to advertising performance, diagnostics, and fraud prevention.

This information may be used to provide personalized or non-personalized ads, measure advertising performance, limit ad frequency, detect invalid activity, and maintain advertising services. Even when non-personalized ads are selected, advertising services may still process information necessary to deliver ads. Actual processing depends on your region, device settings, consent choices, and Google’s advertising configuration.

Where legally required, Beeze requests consent through UMP before requesting ads. You can manage your advertising ID in device settings. When Google requires a privacy options entry point, you can also review or change your choices under “About → Ad privacy options.”

Data processing by Google and its partners is governed by their respective privacy policies. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [How Google uses information from sites or apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 4. System Permissions

Beeze requests the following permissions only when necessary to provide the corresponding features:

### 1. Audio and Storage Access

- On Android 13 and later, Beeze uses the “Music and audio” access permission.
- On Android 12 and earlier, Beeze uses the corresponding storage access permission.
- This access is used to scan, display, and play local audio files on your device.
- If you deny this permission, Beeze will not be able to scan or play your local music.

### 2. Lyrics Folder Access

- Beeze does not request All Files Access.
- To display local lyrics, you can select one or more folders under “About → Lyrics folders.”
- Android’s Storage Access Framework allows Beeze to read only the folders you select, solely to find and read `.lrc` files accompanying your songs.
- You can scan and play music, manage playlists, and search song titles without selecting a lyrics folder.
- You can remove an authorized folder at any time under “About → Lyrics folders.” Beeze will no longer be able to read lyrics from a removed folder.

### 3. Notification Permission

- This permission is used to display media playback notifications, current playback status, and controls for play, pause, and track changes.
- Denying notification permission does not affect normal playback controls within Beeze.

### 4. Foreground Media Playback Service

- This service keeps music playing when you leave the app or lock your screen and provides playback controls through the system media notification.
- It is not used to collect or upload personal information.

## 5. Deletion, Modification, and Restoration

- **Renaming a song:** This changes only the song’s display name within Beeze. It does not change the original audio file’s name or metadata.
- **Deleting a song:** Deleting a local song in Beeze only adds it to the app’s scan exclusion list. It does not delete the actual audio file from your device.
- **Restoring songs and folders:** You can go to “Home → Scan → Manage” to view excluded songs and folders and remove them from the exclusion list when needed.
- **Deleting a playlist:** Deleting a custom playlist does not delete its songs or modify or delete any original audio files.
- **Clearing app data:** You can clear Beeze’s app data through your Android device settings. This also clears local advertising consent state and app-open cooldown records. Data held by Google or advertising partners must be managed separately under their privacy policies.
- **Uninstalling the app:** Uninstalling Beeze removes its locally stored app data but does not delete the original audio or lyrics files on your device.

## 6. Sharing, Transfer, and Public Disclosure

We do not share, transfer, or publicly disclose your local music content, such as songs, lyrics, listening history, liked songs, or custom playlists, with third parties.

Google and its advertising partners may process or share the advertising-related data described in Section 3 to provide ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations. Beeze does not sell your local music content or in-app personal lists.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

You can manage your information and permissions in the following ways:

- Allow or revoke audio, file, and notification permissions in your Android device settings.
- Manage liked songs, custom playlists, and scan exclusion records within Beeze.
- Manage advertising choices through device advertising settings and, where applicable, “About → Ad privacy options.”
- Clear app data or uninstall Beeze to delete the app data stored on your device.

Revoking certain permissions may prevent the corresponding features from working, but it does not affect local processing that was lawfully completed before the permission was revoked.

## 8. Children’s Privacy

Beeze is not specifically directed at children, and the developer does not actively collect children’s personal information. Minors should use the app and read this policy with the guidance and assistance of a parent or guardian. Advertising content and data processing may also be affected by the app’s target-audience and advertising configuration.

## 9. Information Security

Beeze reduces privacy risks by processing music content locally, limiting the purposes for which permissions are used, connecting to advertising network services only when necessary, and disabling cloud backup of app data.

Please note that risks such as device loss, system vulnerabilities, malware, network transmission, or unauthorized device access cannot be fully controlled by the app. We recommend keeping your device’s operating system up to date and protecting your device appropriately.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy in response to feature changes or applicable legal and regulatory requirements. When we do, we will publish the latest version on this page and update the dates at the top.

If an update materially changes the purposes or methods of personal information processing, or the types of information processed, we will notify you through reasonable means, such as an in-app notice, and obtain your consent again where required.
